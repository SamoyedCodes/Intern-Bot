# Application workshop

Mode: Operate. Scope: Intern-Bot's five native macOS destinations.

## Direction contract

**Thesis.** Every application is a body in one orbit chart: its orbit says which stage it has reached, its mark says whose turn it is. The chart replaces a boxed illustration above a table; the queue and the decision sit beside it.

**Own world.** A night-sky chart. Deep navy sky, chalk-white orbit lines, a warm sun for submitted work, amber for anything that needs the student. Navy shell (navigation, inspector, sky) over cool paper in light appearance and navy paper in dark. Futura for titles, the system face for everything you operate.

**Story.** Paste a job and watch its planet start on the outer Intake orbit. Fill and Verify move it inward; a question puts an amber ring on it; Dispatch holds it for approval; only an employer receipt or the student's own confirmation brings it to the inner Applied orbit. Closed applications rest on the rim.

**First viewport.** Navigation strip, title with counts, view segments, search and Run queue, then the job link row. Below, the sky fills the left side with every visible application drawn as a planet; the right column holds filters, the queue table and the deep navy inspector with the one primary action. List view moves the queue into the sky's place.

**Form.** Draft 2 Orbit from `docs/design-drafts/stage/` (seed:2e16e9cc089f0a9b), chosen by the user over Line and Maquette. Signature move: application state drawn as an orbit chart; clicking a planet selects its row, clicking an orbit label explains the station, 3D / Top / Side presets turn the camera.

**Finish.** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
